# Auger Meter V1 — Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Severity | Current treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| AUG-R01 | Fine powder may bridge above the 12 mm hopper throat or refill the screw inconsistently. | High | Phase 02 DEM over friction/cohesion ranges; add inlet agitation only if the evidence requires it. |
| AUG-R02 | Powder may continue to discharge after the screw stops, hurting line endings and corners. | High | Measure stop residual in DEM and compare discharge/nozzle variants. |
| AUG-R03 | Real printed screw/barrel clearance may differ enough from CAD to bind or leak. | Medium | Keep clearance parametric; later run printer/material clearance coupons. |
| AUG-R04 | Powder may migrate toward end bearings and raise drag. | Medium | Bearings are outside the process cavity; sealing/wipers are deferred until the powder simulation is stable. |
| AUG-R05 | A single screw may not meter a highly cohesive rangoli powder consistently. | High | Keep twin-screw and agitation as fallback concepts; promote only if the single-screw design fails the robust DEM envelope. |

No risk above is treated as closed by the Phase 01 geometry checks.
